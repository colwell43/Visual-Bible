About the Visual Bible Project

Each Visual book contains the full text of one book of the Bible, with photographs from everyday life chosen to help you connect your own story with God’s story. The photographs are not pictures of Bible scenes; they are windows for reflection.

Each chapter opens with a short prayer and closes with Master Questions. At the end of each book, a Summary reviews the whole book and Going Deeper offers a short study guide with free online resources. You can write your responses and highlight the text, and everything you write stays on your own device.

  • HEADUnderstand what the passage says and what it meant to its first readers.
  • HEARTNotice your feelings and attitudes before God, and what the passage shows about who He is.
  • ACTIONDecide what you will do in response.
